# Universal Target Lifecycle and Policy Shadow

Lifecycle Shadow combines Availability, Utilization and Performance without collapsing them. It emits a structured explanation containing primary/secondary reasons, confidence, freshness, blockers, next observation and policy preview, plus one recommendation: monitor, recheck, operator identity review, replacement recommendation/preparation, or insufficient evidence.

Policy Shadow applies package-specific interpretation only after package, entitlement, ownership, onboarding, pause, cancel, downgrade, campaign, blacklist and stock gates. Growth and Pro recommend a client target request. Premium recommends automatic replacement preparation. Every output declares `mutationExecuted=false`, `automaticActionAllowed=false`, `notificationSent=false` and `emailSent=false`.

No recommendation is an archive or execution command.
